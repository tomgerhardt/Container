# Design System Document

This document outlines the core aesthetic and functional principles of our design system, derived directly from our technical theme tokens.

## Theme Overview

Our design system is configured for a **dark** color mode, providing a sophisticated and comfortable viewing experience, especially in low-light environments. The overall aesthetic is grounded in a `tonal_spot` color variant, which informs the generated palette.

## Color Palette

The color scheme is meticulously defined to ensure brand consistency and visual hierarchy.

- **Primary Color:** `#957042` - A warm, inviting shade that serves as the dominant brand color, ideal for calls to action, primary buttons, and key interactive elements.
- **Secondary Color:** `#8b7357` - A complementary earthy tone that supports the primary color, suitable for secondary actions, chips, and less prominent UI elements.
- **Tertiary Color:** `#a26953` - An additional accent color, a rich terracotta hue, used for highlights, badges, or decorative elements to add visual interest.
- **Neutral Color:** `#7e766d` - A soft, balanced neutral used extensively for backgrounds, surfaces, and non-chromatic UI components, ensuring legibility and harmony.

## Typography

Our typography system is built to ensure clarity, readability, and a consistent brand voice across all touchpoints. The selected font family offers a contemporary and elegant feel.

- **Headline Font:** `Epilogue` - Used for all headings and prominent display text, chosen for its modern and expressive character.
- **Body Font:** `Epilogue` - Applied to all body text, ensuring excellent readability for longer content blocks.
- **Label Font:** `Epilogue` - Utilized for labels, buttons, and other interactive text elements, maintaining consistency across the UI.

## Shape and Form

The tactile qualities of our user interface elements are defined by their roundedness.

- **Roundedness:** Our components feature a **moderate** level of roundedness (value: `2`), striking a balance between sharpness and softness. This offers a friendly yet professional aesthetic.

## Spacing

The arrangement and proximity of UI elements are governed by our spacing scale, which influences the overall density and readability of our layouts.

- **Spacing:** We utilize a **normal** level of spacing (value: `2`). This provides a comfortable visual breathing room, balancing information density with an organized and clean layout.
